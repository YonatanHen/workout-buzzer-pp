Privacy Policy

Workout Buzzer · Last updated 9 August 2026

Workout Buzzer is an interval timer for training. It has no accounts and no server of its own. Your workouts, settings and history are stored on your phone. The app does send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can see which timers get used and fix what breaks — you can switch the usage statistics off at any time.

This policy explains exactly what is sent, what is not, and what control you have. It is published by Yonatan Hen, the developer of Workout Buzzer.

What the app sends

Usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record what happens inside the app:

  • Which activity you pick, and where you picked it from
  • How a workout is configured — total length, number of rounds, which fields you adjusted
  • Whether a workout was finished or stopped early, how long it ran, and pauses and skips
  • Which screens you open
  • Settings you change, such as theme, language or sound
  • Saving, editing, duplicating and deleting a custom workout
  • Whether your device has a usable text-to-speech voice

These records describe the shape of what you do, never its content:

  • Names you type never leave the phone. Workout names and exercise block names are not sent. When you save a workout, we record that it has (for example) 4 blocks, 8 rounds and 320 seconds of work — not what you called it.
  • Free text cannot be sent at all. Every value in an event is either a number or a word from a fixed list built into the app.
  • Your saved workouts are not individually identified. They are all reported as one category, custom.

Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes, it sends the error and a stack trace, along with the device context that makes a crash diagnosable: operating system version, device model, screen orientation, and memory and storage state. It also records which screen you were on and which activity was selected. It records nothing about you.

Information Google attaches automatically

Google Analytics adds the following to every event and there is no setting that removes them:

  • App version
  • Device model and operating system version
  • Approximate country, derived from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored by us.

Identifiers

There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing is tied to your name or email address. Two pseudonymous identifiers are generated by Firebase:

IdentifierScope
Instance IDOne per app installation. Reset by uninstalling and reinstalling.
Session IDOne per session of app use.

The advertising ID is not collected. Neither is the Android device ID (SSAID) or the iOS vendor ID. These are the identifiers that would let activity be followed between different apps, and they are switched off in the app's configuration. Google Signals — Google's cross-device and cross-site measurement feature — is turned off for this project.

What stays on your phone

Held in the app's own storage and never uploaded:

  • Your settings: language, theme, sound, voice, haptics, keep-screen-awake
  • Your saved custom workouts, including their names and block names
  • Your session history and streak

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

The app keeps the screen awake during a workout, and plays sound, speech and vibration cues. It does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts, your files or your calendar.

Advertising

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will both be updated before that version is released, and people in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent first.

Your choices

Turning usage statistics off. Open Settings in the app and switch off Share usage data. Collection stops immediately, in the app and in the Firebase SDK itself.

Crash reports are not covered by that switch. They are kept on because they contain no record of what you do in the app, and losing them means real faults go unnoticed. If you would prefer not to send them, uninstalling the app is the way to stop it.

How long data is kept

Usage statistics are held by Google for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are held for up to 90 days. We keep no copies anywhere else.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone and stops all further collection.

Because the app never records who you are, statistics already collected cannot be traced back to you, which also means we cannot single them out to delete on request. If you want data connected to your installation removed, write to the address below before uninstalling and include the date and approximate time you last used the app, and we will do what we can. Everything already collected is deleted automatically at the end of the retention period above in any case.

Who else is involved

Google LLC processes the usage statistics and crash reports on our behalf, through Firebase. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy documentation. No one else receives any of it, and none of it is sold.

Google processes this data on servers that may be outside your country, including in the United States.

Legal basis, for users in the EEA and the UK

Usage statistics are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used so it can be improved, and you can object at any time using the switch in Settings. Crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. You have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Requests can be made to the address below, subject to the identification limits described under "Deleting your data".

Children

Workout Buzzer is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 16. If you believe a child has used the app and you want the associated data removed, write to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version appears at this address, and the copy inside the app is updated in the next app release. The date at the top always shows the current version.
